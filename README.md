This was a temporary copy of the main app, served at https://tchev45.github.io/my-flra-test/ while flra.pricedev.ca was blocked on the company iPads.
It was taken down on 2026-11-22. The app lives at https://flra.pricedev.ca (repo TCHEV45/flra). Do not publish builds here.

`sw.js` is all that is left on purpose: it removes the cached app from devices that had this copy installed and then removes itself. It does not touch forms saved on the device.
